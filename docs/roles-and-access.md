# Roles And Access Model

This document describes the target university product, not a claim that role management is implemented. It expands the decisions recorded at the [Week 5 Customer meeting](../reports/week-05/meeting-report.md#decisions). The existing learner increment stays accepted; these rules describe the next access and teaching capabilities.

**Status:** Draft for team review, grounded in recorded decisions. Confirmed rules cite their source below. Proposed enforcement details and open policy are labelled separately; this document does not create a Customer decision.

## Scope And Sources

- [DEC-017](decisions.md#dec-017): three roles, administrator-controlled teacher accounts and teacher-only public publication.
- [DEC-018](decisions.md#dec-018): teacher/student generation, personal keys and teacher-only use of the administrator's key.
- [DEC-019](decisions.md#dec-019): university-email authorization and no anonymous/public access for now.
- [DEC-020](decisions.md#dec-020): the team's private/public/group audience model and one exercise list.
- [DEC-021](decisions.md#dec-021): named groups, one teacher per group and group results as CSV without grades.
- [DEC-022](decisions.md#dec-022): a deadline for each group/exercise pair and the best submission.
- [DEC-026](decisions.md#dec-026): university-first scope without paid features or Moodle integration.

The imposed constraints live in the [product vision](product-vision.md#constraints). [US-09](https://github.com/team-ok-ITPD/team-OK-itpd/issues/111) covers audience-controlled publication. Group administration, role administration and deadlines need their own story/specification work before implementation is treated as complete; this document is not a replacement for those records.

## Role Profiles

### Administrator

A system-provided account that creates or changes teacher accounts and supplies a shared generation API key for teachers. It is not an exercise-generation role and does not inherit teacher publication rights merely because it administers the service ([DEC-017](decisions.md#dec-017), [DEC-018](decisions.md#dec-018)).

Administrator provisioning/recovery, the number of administrator accounts and access to learner submissions are not specified. Do not treat Django superuser access to the database as an agreed product capability.

### Teacher

A teaching user whose account is created or changed only by the administrator. A teacher can generate exercises using a personal key or the administrator's shared key, publish exercises, create named groups and download results for their groups. The team's audience model also lets a teacher keep an exercise private or share it with a group ([DEC-017](decisions.md#dec-017) through [DEC-021](decisions.md#dec-021)).

Each group has one teacher. That does not set a limit on how many groups a teacher may have. Teacher-to-teacher access and whether a teacher can also hold the student role remain open.

### Student

A learner who registers using a university email, practices accessible exercises, and can generate private exercises using a personal API key. A student cannot publish to the public gallery or use the administrator's shared key. An exercise generated by someone else can be used without the student supplying a generation key, provided access is allowed ([DEC-017](decisions.md#dec-017) through [DEC-020](decisions.md#dec-020)).

How students join a group and whether they may share their own exercise with a group have not been agreed. Their group membership is separate from their account role.

"Open" is not an intended permission. The proposed implementation default below denies operations without an agreed grant. Teacher access to publishing does not imply ownership of every teacher's exercises; that scope needs confirmation.

## Access And Data Constraints

### Identity And Roles

The target has three product roles. Only the administrator creates or changes teacher accounts. University-email authorization is required, but the accepted email domains, verification mechanism and administrator bootstrap path remain open. A client choosing a role during registration must not be treated as authority to become a teacher or administrator.

### Exercise Audience

The team's [DEC-020](decisions.md#dec-020) model records an author and one of three audiences: private, public or group. A private exercise is intended for its author; public access still requires university sign-in; a group exercise is intended for the selected group's members. Administrative override and cross-teacher access are not settled.

The My, Public, Group and All filters select from exercises the user is already permitted to access. A filter or a copied link is not an access grant. Only teachers can publish publicly. The mechanism for promoting generated content into shared content needs authorization checks of its own.

### Groups And Assignments

A group has a name and exactly one teacher. Membership, exercise ownership and account role are distinct relationships. Assigning one exercise to two groups produces two group/exercise assignments; their deadlines must remain independent.

The entry/exit process, group naming uniqueness, deletion/archival, ownership transfer and effects of removing a student or teacher are unresolved. Do not assume shared group URLs provide enrollment or access.

### Submissions And Results

[DEC-022](decisions.md#dec-022) selects the best submission rather than the last edit. [DEC-021](decisions.md#dec-021) requires a teacher's group CSV to show who solved which exercise and how many tests passed, without grades or aggregate statistics.

The best-submission ordering, tie-breaking, treatment of changed test sets and late submissions need agreement. Do not invent a grade, an automatic last-edit submission or a timezone convention. Retention and removal of results after membership changes are also open.

### API Keys

Generation and practice are different permissions: practicing someone else's accessible exercise does not itself require a personal generation key. Teacher/student personal keys and the administrator-supplied teacher key have different permitted users. Raw-key visibility, storage, revocation, quotas and billing limits have not been agreed; using a shared key does not grant the user the right to read or export its secret value.

### Product Boundary

The current direction is a university teaching service. Moodle integration, paid features, grades and aggregate CSV statistics are outside the recorded scope. Existing accepted learner behavior is not reopened merely because these new capabilities are specified.

## Proposed Enforcement Rules

These are implementation proposals for review, not additional Customer decisions or completed features:

- Deny operations without an explicit permission; verify role, ownership, audience and group relationship on the server for every relevant request, including direct URLs and exports.
- Treat role changes as privileged server operations. Registration payloads, hidden buttons and Django's generic staff flag are not the business role model.
- Apply the same audience rules to exercise lists, details, workspace creation, files and result endpoints; an IDE token must not broaden the user's exercise access.
- Restrict teacher group exports and assignment changes to their own groups until a different policy is approved. Treat other users' drafts and API secrets as inaccessible by default while their inspection policy is unresolved.
- Keep API secret values out of browser responses, exports and logs; grant use of a configured key separately from permission to replace it. The shared key may be configured only through an administrator-authorized action.
- Changing a group deadline must not change another group's assignment or the underlying exercise. Check result eligibility and the deadline at submission on the server; choose a late-submission and timezone policy before implementing it.

The last two rules specify proposed key-management and deadline enforcement; they do not settle quota, storage or late-submission policy.

## Open Questions Before Implementation

1. Can a teacher also act as a student, or are roles exclusive? Can the administrator perform teaching/practice actions beyond the explicit grants?
2. Which university domains are allowed, how is email ownership verified, and how is the fixed administrator provisioned and recovered?
3. Who enrolls/removes students: a teacher, an invitation, a join request or another process? Can a student belong to several groups?
4. Who may inspect source/drafts, share a student's exercise, or access another teacher's exercises and group results?
5. What happens to memberships, assignments, exercises and historical results when a teacher/group is removed or ownership changes?
6. What is the precise best-submission order, deadline timezone, tie-break and late-submission rule? Can an exercise or its default tests change after submissions exist?
7. How are keys stored, rotated and limited, who sees usage, and what happens when a shared key is exhausted?

## Current Implementation And Next Records

The Django app has ordinary user accounts, an admin site and an `is_staff` check for creating exercises. Drafts, attempts and IDE tokens are linked to individual users. It has no distinct teacher/student model, teaching-group membership, group/exercise assignments or complete audience/role enforcement. The current staff check is not proof that the target matrix is implemented.

Before implementation, refine the appropriate backlog stories, record measurable FR criteria and choose the necessary data/access design. Keep proposed policy separate until it is confirmed. Do not rewrite the accepted US-01 through US-05 or claim this documentation task delivers group or role management.
