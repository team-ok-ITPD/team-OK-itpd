# QR-07: Learners Can Operate The Debugging Workflow

A representative learner can complete the prepared debugging workflow with an ease rating that supports independent use.

- **Status:** Active
- **Characteristic:** Interaction Capability
- **Sub-characteristic:** Operability
- **Business importance:** High
- **Technical risk:** Medium

Operability is measured because the session asks whether learners can carry out the main workflow without the facilitator operating it for them. The main task's SEQ reads that perceived ease immediately after the workflow.

## Scenario

### Source

A representative adult learner matching [UT-01's participant profile](../../usability-tests/ut-01/README.md#participant-profile).

### Stimulus

The learner completes UT-01's main prepared-debugging-exercise task.

### Environment

The staging deployment on the university network with synthetic seeded data and no real payment.

### Artifact

The prepared exercise, its workspace and verification flow described by [UT-01](../../usability-tests/ut-01/README.md).

### Response

The product shall let the learner carry out the main workflow and rate its ease after the task.

### Response Measure

For at least three counted UT-01 participants, the median main-task SEQ is at least 5 on the seven-point scale. This pre-session bar distinguishes an easy-enough workflow from one that needs usability rework.

## QRT-01

- **Status:** Active
- **Level:** manual
- **Runs at:** [UT-01](../../usability-tests/ut-01/README.md) and its [results](../../usability-tests/ut-01/results.md).
- **Why Manual:** A command cannot judge a participant's reported ease after the main task or the help they required. This test reads no SLI.

### Procedure

1. Run UT-01's main task with at least three counted participants using its protocol.
2. Record each participant's main-task SEQ and calculate the median in `results.md`.
3. Preserve the private raw survey records and publish only the sanitized aggregate result.

**Pass when:** at least three counted participants have a recorded main-task SEQ and their median is at least 5 of 7.
