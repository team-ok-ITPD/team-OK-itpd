# QR-04: A Learner Reaches The Exercise Quickly

A learner who asks for an exercise in the catalog sees the answer within two seconds and has the exercise open in the workspace within 30 seconds.

- **Status:** Active
- **Characteristic:** Performance Efficiency
- **Sub-characteristic:** Time Behaviour
- **Business importance:** Medium
- **Technical risk:** Medium

## Scenario

### Source

A learner who wants to practise a kind of bug.

### Stimulus

The learner submits a plain-language request for a supported exercise in the catalog and opens the workspace it links.

### Environment

The product is running on the learner's own mid-range laptop with its images already built, and the learner uses a current browser, as the clean-checkout path in [the setup guide](../../setup.md) describes.

### Artifact

The catalog and the browser-based workspace.

### Response

The catalog shall return the exercise and the workspace link without waiting for the learner to do anything else, and the workspace shall open with the exercise files and the debug configuration ready.

### Response Measure

In at least nine of ten attempts, the catalog answers within two seconds and the workspace shows the exercise files within 30 seconds of the learner opening it.
