# Threshold of success

The targets below are the team's Week 3 draft. They remain open proposals until
the Customer reviews the measures; any agreed change will be recorded with a
customer decision.

## TOS-01

At least three pilot learners complete one Python debugging exercise in 20 minutes or less without installing local language tooling by the end of Week 5.

- **Status:** Open
- **Measure:** three dated pilot records showing the learner opened the prepared
  environment, reproduced the bug, used the debugger, fixed the code, and passed
  the tests in 20 minutes or less by the end of Week 5.

## TOS-02

At least nine of ten sampled exercises contain the requested bug and tests that pass on the correct program and fail on the buggy program by the end of Week 7.

- **Status:** Open
- **Measure:** a repeatable validation run over ten sampled exercises, with at
  least nine passing both checks, recorded by the end of Week 7.

## TOS-03

The Customer accepts the complete VS Code debugging workflow and at least four of five pilot learners report that the prepared environment helped them practise debugging by the end of Week 9.

- **Status:** Open
- **Measure:** a linked Customer acceptance decision and at least four positive
  responses from five pilot learners to the same usefulness question, recorded
  by the end of Week 9.

## Failure And Success Statements

These statements were drafted by the team on 2026-10-17 and still require
Customer review.

| Goal | Success statement | Failure statement |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [TOS-01](#tos-01) | Learners reach a working exercise quickly and complete the full debugging loop. | Learners spend the session setting up tools or cannot complete the core task. |
| [TOS-02](#tos-02) | Exercises reliably contain the requested defect and prove it with executable tests. | Generated exercises contain the wrong defect or tests that do not distinguish correct and buggy programs. |
| [TOS-03](#tos-03) | The Customer and pilot learners find the VS Code workflow suitable for realistic debugging practice. | The workflow is not accepted or learners do not find the prepared environment useful. |

## Sprint 9 Evidence Reconciliation

The retrospective [Sprint 9 review record](../reports/week-09/meeting-report.md)
contains positive student-workflow feedback and an explicit passing statement
for a demonstrated student UAT. It does not supply the three timely Week 5
debugger records required by TOS-01, the ten-exercise validation required by
TOS-02, or the five comparable learner usefulness responses plus complete
workflow acceptance required by TOS-03.

Achieved/Missed verdict against dated evidence. The inspected recording alone
cannot settle earlier runs, and the maintained record still calls the measures
draft proposals. Their existing Open values are unresolved record gaps, not an
