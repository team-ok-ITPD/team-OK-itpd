## Session Script

1. Welcome the participant, explain that the product is being tested, not them, and remind them they may stop at any time.
2. Confirm the completed consent, the age declaration and the recording permission before starting any recording.
3. Collect the pre-test from [the surveys](../surveys.md) privately and assign the next `P-nn` code.
4. Ask the participant to think aloud: say what they expect, notice and decide; silence is acceptable.
5. Read each scenario exactly, start the timer, observe without help, record outcome, help, time and friction, then ask the SEQ question.
6. After Task 3, collect the SUS and the open-ended post-test answers privately.
7. Debrief, explain the next steps, and confirm the withdrawal contact information.

## No-Help Rule

The facilitator reads the scenario and does nothing else: no teaching, correcting, demonstrating or pointing at a screen, control or step. Help is a navigation, interpretation or technical hint beyond repeating the scenario. When the participant asks for help, the facilitator may give one neutral, minimum hint and the observer records the request and the hint; a second hint ends the task as `Assisted`. Silence, a clarifying question about the scenario's wording and a reminder of the time limit are not help.

## Stop Rule

Stop and do not count the session on withdrawal, refused recording, missing consent, the participant asking to stop, or a material technical failure. Remove the raw text and the recording of a withdrawn session, retire its code, and recruit a replacement under the next free code.

## Dry Runs

A dry run with a teammate checks the wording, the time limits and the instrumentation. It is not evidence and is never counted, and it receives no `P-nn` code. No dry run has been run yet; the team records each one here as not counted when it happens.
