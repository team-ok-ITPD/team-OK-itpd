# FR-008: Sign In With A Student Or Professor Role

- **Status:** Active
- **Traces to:** [`DEC-017`](../../decisions.md#dec-017), [`DEC-019`](../../decisions.md#dec-019), [`roles-and-access`](../../roles-and-access.md)
- **Containers:** `exercise catalog`

## Behavior

When a person registers with a username, an email and a password, the product shall create an account with the `student` role and sign the person in.

When a signed-out person requests a catalog or learner API route that needs an account, the product shall answer `401`.

When a student requests a professor-only teaching route, the product shall answer `403`.

The product shall expose the signed-in account's username, email and role to its own pages.

## Acceptance Criteria

### AC-01

Given no account, when a person registers with a username, email and password, then the response succeeds and the account's role is `student`.

### AC-02

Given a signed-out request to `/api/history/`, then the response status is `401`.

### AC-03

Given a signed-in student, when the student requests `/api/teach/groups/`, then the response status is `403`.

## Open points

The implementation names the teacher role `professor` and has no administrator role, while [DEC-017](../../decisions.md#dec-017) names `teacher` and `administrator`. Enforcing university-email registration ([DEC-019](../../decisions.md#dec-019)) and professor provisioning are not specified here; the team decides them before this record claims them.
