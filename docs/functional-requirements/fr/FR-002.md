# FR-002: Open A Ready Workspace

- **Status:** Active
- **Traces to:** [`US-02`](https://github.com/team-ok-ITPD/team-OK-itpd/issues/29), [`DEC-005`](../../decisions.md#dec-005), [`CON-02`](../../product-vision.md#con-02)
- **Containers:** `exercise catalog`, `debug workspace`

## Behavior

When a matching exercise is requested and its workspace is healthy, the product shall provide a workspace containing the broken code, its dependencies, its tests, and its debugger configuration without requiring the learner to install language tooling.

When workspace preparation fails, the product shall report the failure, shall remove any partially prepared workspace, and shall not link that workspace to the learner.

## Acceptance Criteria

### AC-01

Given a healthy workspace, when a supported exercise is requested, then opening the returned link shows `remove_negatives.py`, its pytest tests, and the `Debug reproduction tests` launch configuration.

### AC-02

Given a workspace health endpoint that returns `503`, when a supported exercise is requested, then the catalog returns `503`, explains that the workspace is unavailable, returns no exercise object, and leaves no newly prepared workspace state.
