# FR-009: Control An Exercise's Audience

- **Status:** Active
- **Traces to:** [`US-09`](https://github.com/team-ok-ITPD/team-OK-itpd/issues/111), [`DEC-017`](../../decisions.md#dec-017), [`DEC-020`](../../decisions.md#dec-020), [`roles-and-access`](../../roles-and-access.md#exercise-audience)
- **Containers:** `exercise catalog`

## Behavior

The product shall give each prepared exercise exactly one audience: `private`, `public` or `group`.

When a professor sets the audience of an exercise they own, the product shall store it. When the audience is `group` and the exercise has no group assignment, the product shall refuse the change.

When a learner lists or opens exercises, the product shall return only exercises that learner may open: their own, public ones, and ones assigned to a group they belong to. A copied link shall not grant access.

## Acceptance Criteria

### AC-01

Given a professor's private exercise, when a different signed-in student requests its detail, then the response is `404`.

### AC-02

Given an exercise with no group assignment, when its owner sets the audience to `group`, then the response status is `400` and the audience is unchanged.

### AC-03

Given a public exercise, when any signed-in student lists exercises, then the exercise appears in the list.
