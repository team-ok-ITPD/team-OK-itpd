# FR-010: Assign An Exercise To Groups With A Deadline

- **Status:** Active
- **Traces to:** [`DEC-021`](../../decisions.md#dec-021), [`DEC-022`](../../decisions.md#dec-022), [`DEC-033`](../../decisions.md#dec-033), [`DEC-035`](../../decisions.md#dec-035)
- **Containers:** `exercise catalog`

## Behavior

When a professor assigns an exercise they own to one or more of their own groups with an optional deadline, the product shall store one assignment per group and exercise with that group's own deadline. Repeating the assignment shall update the deadline and not duplicate it.

The product shall refuse an assignment that names no group, a group the professor does not teach, or a deadline that is not an ISO date.

When a student requests their assignments, the product shall list only assignments for groups the student belongs to.

## Acceptance Criteria

### AC-01

Given a professor's group and exercise, when the professor assigns it twice with different deadlines, then one assignment exists and carries the second deadline.

### AC-02

Given a group taught by another professor, when the professor assigns an exercise to it, then the response status is `400` and no assignment is created.

### AC-03

Given a student in one group, when the student requests `/api/assignments/`, then only that group's assignments are returned.

## Open points

Group creation and membership administration, and CSV export of group results ([DEC-021](../../decisions.md#dec-021)), have no specification yet.
