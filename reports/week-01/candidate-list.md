# Candidate List

This is the full candidate set considered for Week 1. The first three were selected for detailed comparison because together they represent a focused debugging product, a broad practice platform, and an open-source research environment. The remaining candidates were cut from detailed comparison, not judged irrelevant.

| Candidate | URL | Relevance | Decision |
|---|---|---|---|
| BugHunt | https://www.trybughunt.com/ | Debugging tasks focused on finding and fixing bugs in existing code. | Selected: direct debugging-practice product. |
| Codewars | https://www.codewars.com/kata?tags=Debugging | Includes predefined debugging challenges within a broader coding-practice platform. | Selected: adjacent practice-platform comparison. |
| Microsoft debug-gym | https://github.com/microsoft/debug-gym | Interactive, repository-level debugging environment for AI-agent research. | Selected: open-source and extensible environment. |
| SWE-bench | https://www.swebench.com/original.html | Uses real GitHub issues as reproducible software-engineering tasks. | Cut: benchmark/task dataset rather than a learner-facing debugging product. |
| BuLyst | https://buglyst.com/ | Focuses on realistic debugging workflows. | Cut: overlapping debugging-practice focus; not prioritized over BugHunt for the limited detailed comparison. |
| SadhanAI | https://sadhanai.com/ | Relevant to AI-supported or personalized debugging practice. | Cut: AI assistance is a narrower comparison angle than the selected set's product and environment coverage. |
| Moss | https://www.learnmoss.com/ | Focuses on realistic software-engineering tasks and existing codebases. | Cut: broader software-engineering task focus; less directly centered on debugging practice in the initial screening. |
| Before You Ask for Help | https://www.beforeyouaskforhelp.co.uk/ | Focuses on the debugging process and help-seeking workflow. | Cut: process guidance rather than a comparable interactive task platform. |
| Recticode | https://www.recticode.com/ | Provides software-development and debugging practice scenarios. | Cut: overlaps with practice platforms; not prioritized over the more contrasting selected products. |
| Codritium | https://www.codritium.com/ | Relevant to realistic development scenarios and AI-assisted software engineering. | Cut: broader software-engineering scope; less directly comparable to the selected debugging-focused products. |

The selected alternatives are documented in [`alternatives.md`](../../docs/research/alternatives.md) and compared in [`comparison.md`](../../docs/research/comparison.md). Cut decisions are based on initial relevance and comparison coverage; they do not claim a complete evaluation of the cut candidates.
