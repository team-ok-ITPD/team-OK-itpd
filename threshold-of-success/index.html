Threshold of success

The targets below are the team's Week 3 draft. They remain open proposals until the Customer reviews the measures; any agreed change will be recorded with a customer decision.

TOS-01

At least three pilot learners complete one Python debugging exercise in 20 minutes or less without installing local language tooling by the end of Week 5.

  • Status: Open
  • Measure: three dated pilot records showing the learner opened the prepared environment, reproduced the bug, used the debugger, fixed the code, and passed the tests in 20 minutes or less by the end of Week 5.

TOS-02

At least nine of ten sampled exercises contain the requested bug and tests that pass on the correct program and fail on the buggy program by the end of Week 7.

  • Status: Open
  • Measure: a repeatable validation run over ten sampled exercises, with at least nine passing both checks, recorded by the end of Week 7.

TOS-03

The Customer accepts the complete VS Code debugging workflow and at least four of five pilot learners report that the prepared environment helped them practise debugging by the end of Week 9.

  • Status: Open
  • Measure: a linked Customer acceptance decision and at least four positive responses from five pilot learners to the same usefulness question, recorded by the end of Week 9.

Failure And Success Statements

These statements were drafted by the team on 2026-10-17 and still require Customer review.

Goal Success statement Failure statement
TOS-01 Learners reach a working exercise quickly and complete the full debugging loop. Learners spend the session setting up tools or cannot complete the core task.
TOS-02 Exercises reliably contain the requested defect and prove it with executable tests. Generated exercises contain the wrong defect or tests that do not distinguish correct and buggy programs.
TOS-03 The Customer and pilot learners find the VS Code workflow suitable for realistic debugging practice. The workflow is not accepted or learners do not find the prepared environment useful.
